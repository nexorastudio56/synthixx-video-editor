Most people still use AI like a search box. A chatbot answers. An agent acts. Give it a goal, and it breaks that goal into steps. Then it picks the right tools: search the web, read a file, write code. After every step, it checks its own result. If something fails, it tries a different path. Plan, act, check, repeat. That loop turns a language model into a worker. You stop typing prompts. You start handing off outcomes. That is the shift from asking to delegating.
