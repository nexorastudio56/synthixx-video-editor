## 🏢 About Synthixx Technologies Private Limited

**Synthixx Technologies Private Limited** is a technology company dedicated to building next-generation AI-powered video production tools and creative technology solutions.

Founded by **Jawad Zaheer Kyani**, Synthixx Technologies is on a mission to democratize video production by making it **50× faster** than any existing tool, while maintaining cinematic quality and professional standards.

### Mission
> To empower creators, businesses, and developers worldwide with the fastest, most powerful AI video production engine — making professional video creation accessible to everyone, regardless of technical skill.

### Vision
> A world where anyone can create cinematic, professional-quality videos in minutes, not hours — powered by AI, driven by code, and accessible to all.

## 🌟 Synthixx Video Editor

**Synthixx Video Editor** is the flagship product of Synthixx Technologies Private Limited.

### What Makes It Special

| Feature | Synthixx Video Editor |
|---------|----------------------|
| **Speed** | 50× faster than any other video tool |
| **Quality** | Single-pass lossless (no quality degradation) |
| **Motion Graphics** | Advanced 3D, parallax, kinetic typography, GSAP |
| **Audio** | TTS + WhisperX + Procedural SFX + Voice ducking |
| **Assets** | Real photos, official logos (simple-icons), no cartoons |
| **Pipeline** | 13 stages with validation gates + crash-resume |
| **Themes** | Brand theming (any brand = one token object) |
| **Platforms** | Windows + Linux, Studio UI on localhost:3000 |

### Technology Stack

- **Runtime**: Node.js, TypeScript
- **Rendering**: Playwright + ffmpeg (single-pass pipe)
- **Audio**: Kokoro TTS, SAPI, Edge TTS, WhisperX
- **Visuals**: GSAP, Three.js, Sharp, Simple Icons
- **Schemas**: Zod (v1 Scene Graph)
- **UI**: React, esbuild (Studio on localhost:3000)
