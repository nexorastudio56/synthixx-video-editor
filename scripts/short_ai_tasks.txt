Most small business owners use AI wrong. They open a chatbot, type one question, and close it. That is like buying a truck and only using the radio.
Here is a better way. Think in tasks, not questions. A task has an input, a clear result, and a person who checks it.
Task one: replies. Paste a customer message, and let AI draft the answer in your tone. You read it, fix one line, and send. Two minutes, not twenty.
Task two: follow ups. Every quote you sent last week and never heard back about. Give AI the list, and it writes a short, friendly nudge for each one.
Task three: your week. Dump every messy note from your phone into AI and ask for a plan. Three priorities, one risk, and what to ignore.
Start small. Pick one task today, and run it for a week. If it saves you thirty minutes, keep it. If not, drop it.
